01The problem

Generating an answer is easy. Finding the right authority is the hard part.

Wisconsin property-tax guidance is spread across the Constitution, statutes, case law, administrative rules, the assessment manual (WPAM), FAQs, government publications and professional standards. A flat vector index loses that structure: it can’t tell a statute from a FAQ that paraphrases it.

The DOR wanted assessment staff to ask questions in plain English and get answers grounded in the right level of authority, with every source a click away.

02System

Research first, then write.

An authenticated question becomes an EventBridge event. In Phase A, Claude Sonnet runs a 14-tool research loop over Neptune Analytics, combining vector search with traversal of cites, implements and supersedes edges across the authority hierarchy. A Haiku adequacy judge then decides whether to answer, ask a clarifying question (with suggestion chips) or decline. In Phase B, the answer streams over WebSocket while citations are repaired and any invented URLs are stripped.

03My part

I led the move from a legacy vector search to GraphRAG, then took it to production.

  1. Grounding that can’t be faked. If the answer cites a statute section that was never retrieved, the system fetches it. Citations are repaired before they stream, and invented URLs never reach the user.
  2. An eval system with teeth. A graph-regression harness graded by an LLM judge against testers’ real complaints, plus a Phase-B replay that cut a full baseline run from about $10 and 28 minutes to about $1.
  3. Closing the feedback loop. Testers left 73 rated answers, 28 of them mixed or negative. I traced each one to its root cause using the stored retrieval traces and shipped the fixes.
  4. Ingestion that understands legal documents. Dedicated chunkers for admin rules, WPAM and case law, statute aliases and subsection splitting, docket-keyed case-law dedup, and a ten-phase graph load on ECS Fargate.
  5. A real handoff. Rebuilt the cost model from the actual bill, which showed the earlier estimate was 2–3× too high. Also added blue/green Neptune graph swaps and a post-audit security pass: TLS-only buckets, scoped IAM, cdk-nag and cost alarms.
  6. An admin trace replay. An activity dashboard that replays each stored retrieval trace with a pane per tool, so the team can see why an answer happened instead of guessing.
