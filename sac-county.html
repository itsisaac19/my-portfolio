01The problem

Trust is the whole product.

Prosecutors need fast, accurate answers about a single case: who said what, when, and where it’s written down. Anything an AI says has to be traceable to a page in that case’s file, or it’s worse than useless.

The constraints were hard ones. Source documents can’t be copied out of the county’s case-management storage, everything has to run in AWS GovCloud, and the assistant must stay within the office’s ethics line on AI: it finds, quotes and cites. It never recommends.

Find. Quote. Cite. Never recommend.
02System

From change feed to cited answer, without leaving GovCloud.

A change feed from the case-management system, plus an “index now” action and a reconcile when a case is opened, kicks off a Step Functions ingest of six Lambda stages: OCR, chunking, embeddings and entity extraction. Everything lands in a graph-shaped Postgres schema. At question time, a Bedrock agent works over tools scoped to that case and streams a cited answer over WebSocket. Every model and tool call records its tokens, cost and latency.

03Decisions

Seven ADRs. Each one closes off a way the system could fail.

  1. Index by content, not by case. Documents are keyed by content hash in a many-to-many index, with case membership owned upstream and applied at query time. Re-linking a file never triggers reprocessing, and duplicates are processed once.
  2. Replace S3 triggers with a change feed. Upstream deletes turned out to be soft and object metadata is frozen at upload, so bucket events would have lied. A change feed plus reconcile-on-open keeps the index honest.
  3. Postgres as the graph. Compared Neptune, Neptune Analytics, Bedrock KB GraphRAG and Aurora pgvector against what GovCloud actually offers. Chose a graph-shaped schema (chunks, entities, mentions and relations, with every edge citable) behind a GraphStore interface.
  4. Events are claims, not facts. Timeline entries carry a time range, a confidence and the source they came from, so contradictory accounts show up side by side instead of being averaged away.
  5. Ship while access is pending. Turned model data retention off on all 20 GovCloud models, and kept development moving on Nova and open models while access to Claude was still being approved.
